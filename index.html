Quantifying & Analyzing Risk (Code)

A 5×5 matrix prioritizes; quantitative analysis puts it in dollars. This project pairs the qualitative register with a FAIR-style Monte Carlo loss model and SQL/pandas analytics — the data-engineering side of GRC that turns a heat map into board-ready, defensible numbers.
